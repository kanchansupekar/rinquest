Rinquest is a UK platform where grassroots players and teams find each other. Players and parents build profiles, teams search by position, level, location and availability, and both sides talk directly, so squads fill faster and players get seen.

How Rinquest works

Two sides of the same match. Both start free.

If you play

  1. Build your profile

    Your sport, position, level, availability and ambitions.

  2. Get discovered

    Coaches and teams searching for players like you can find you.

  3. Find your next team

    Message teams directly and explore the opportunities that suit you.

If you run a team

  1. Create your team profile

    Show players what makes your team a good place to play.

  2. Find the right players

    Search and filter player profiles to match what your squad needs.

  3. Grow your squad

    Connect directly with players and parents to recruit faster.

What makes the match better

  • Verified profiles

    Profiles teams and players can trust. See how verification works.

  • Smart matching

    Opportunities based on level, location, availability and ambition, so you spend time on real fits.

  • Direct connections

    Message the right people directly and start the conversation the same day.

  • Built for families

    Parents can create and manage a profile for their child. Read the parents' guide.
