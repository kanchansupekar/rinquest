Football questions

Can I find youth football teams on Rinquest?

Yes. Parents can create and manage a profile for their child, and teams can look for players across age groups.

Do I need to be in a league to join?

No. Rinquest is built for grassroots football, from Sunday leagues to junior clubs. Teams say what level they play at, and players say what level they are.
