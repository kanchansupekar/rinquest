Find the right players faster

Smarter ways to identify players who match your team's level, availability and culture.

Most squads do not need "a good player". They need a specific gap filled by someone who can actually make training and matches. Recruiting works faster when you start there.

Write down the gap before you search

Name the position, the age group and the level you play at. A clear brief stops you scrolling through profiles that were never going to fit.

Check availability first

A brilliant player who works Saturdays is not a signing. Filter by availability early and you will save yourself several conversations that go nowhere.

Be honest about your level

Tell players what your team really is: relaxed Sunday league, competitive, or somewhere in between. Players who read that and still get in touch are far more likely to stay.

Message early and keep it short

Introduce the team, say what you are looking for and suggest a short chat or a taster session. A quick, specific message gets replies.

Keep a shortlist

Save the profiles you like so that when someone drops out mid-season you already know who to call.
