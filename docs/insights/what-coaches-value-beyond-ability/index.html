What coaches value beyond ability

Technical skill matters, but attitude, commitment, reliability and team fit often make the difference.

At grassroots level, most players are within touching distance of each other on ability. What separates the ones who stay in a squad is usually everything else.

Attitude

Coaches remember who encourages teammates and who sulks after a substitution. Players who are easy to coach get more minutes and more chances.

Commitment and reliability

Turning up on time, telling the manager early when you cannot make it, and being there in week ten as well as week one all count for a lot.

Team fit

Some sides are intense, some are social, most are a blend. A player who fits the culture makes the whole squad better.

How to show it on your profile

  • State your availability clearly and keep it up to date.
  • Describe what you want from a team in your own words.
  • Mention past teams and roles, such as captain, vice-captain or club volunteer.
