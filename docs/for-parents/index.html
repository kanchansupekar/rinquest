Help your child be seen, and stay in charge

Create and manage your child's profile from your own account, so the right teams can find them and you decide what happens next.

Create free profileRead our safeguarding approach

  1. Sign up as a parent

    One account for you. Add a profile for each child who plays.

  2. Add your child's profile

    Sport, position, level and availability. Manage or edit it any time from your account.

  3. Connect with teams

    Teams looking for players like your child can get in touch, and you can explore the opportunities that suit their level and goals.

For families who play more than one sport

Many children split their week between sports. Rinquest supports football, cricket, rugby and hockey, so you can look for the right fit in each.
