The trusted platform for grassroots talent discovery

Rinquest helps players and teams connect through verified profiles, smarter matching and direct communication.

The idea for Rinquest came from firsthand experience in grassroots sport: seeing talented players struggle to reach the right opportunities, while coaches and teams faced the same challenge finding the right fit.

Too often, recruitment has depended on fragmented networks and informal connections rather than transparent, merit-based discovery. Rinquest was built to change that.

Our mission is simple: to help players find the right opportunities and help teams build stronger squads through better matches.

What we stand for

  • Better opportunities

  • Smarter recruitment

    Helping teams identify the right players through transparent, efficient matching.

  • Trusted connections

    A more reliable, merit-based recruitment experience for everyone involved.
